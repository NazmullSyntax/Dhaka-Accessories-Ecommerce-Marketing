# Reels Strategy (Simulated)

**Disclaimer:** Simulated strategy for portfolio purposes.

## Why Reels
Facebook and Instagram prioritize short video in 2024–25. A reel reaches 3–5x more accounts than a static image for the same effort. For e-commerce, video builds trust — customers see the product move, which reduces hesitation.

## The 3-Part Structure
| Part | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 sec | Stop the scroll |
| Body | 3–15 sec | Show product, state benefit |
| CTA | last 3–5 sec | Tell viewer what to do |

## 5 Reel Types We Produce
1. Product showcase — 15 sec
2. Unboxing — 20–30 sec
3. Styling — 25 sec
4. Customer review — 25 sec
5. Sale countdown — 15 sec

## Technical Rules
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (1080 × 1920)
- Length: 15–30 sec (max 45)
- Text overlay: at least 3 per reel
- Safe margin: 100 px from top and bottom
- Music: trending audio from CapCut library
- Volume: music 30–50%, voiceover dominant

## Posting Cadence
| Platform | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Facebook Reels | 2 per week |
| Instagram Reels | 3 per week |
| TikTok | 1 per week |

## Caption Template
Hook line + 2-line body + CTA + 5 hashtags

## Hashtag Strategy
- 5 hashtags per reel

## Posting Time
