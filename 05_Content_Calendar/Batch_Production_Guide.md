# Batch Production Guide (Simulated)

**Disclaimer:** Simulated workflow for portfolio purposes.

## What is Batch Production
Producing all content for one week in a single session, instead of designing every day.

## Weekly Batch Workflow
| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Write all 5 captions for the week | 60 min |
| Sunday | Design 5 Canva creatives | 90 min |
| Monday | Schedule everything in Meta Business Suite | 30 min |
| Tuesday | Prepare 2 stories per day | 20 min |

## Weekly Cadence (Minimum)
- 5 feed posts per week
- 2 stories per day
- 1 reel per week
- 1 engagement post (poll/question)

## Monthly Campaign Rhythm
| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Brand awareness |
| Week 2 | Product showcase + educate |
| Week 3 | Social proof + retargeting |
| Week 4 | Seasonal campaign (11.11, Eid) |

## Tools in the Workflow
- **Google Sheets** — caption planning
- **Canva** — design
- **Meta Business Suite** — scheduling
- **Google Drive** — backup of all files

## Quality Check Before Posting
- [ ] Caption has hook, body, CTA
- [ ] At least one number (price/days)
- [ ] Brand colors used
- [ ] One clear CTA
- [ ] Bangla spelling checked
- [ ] Hashtags 4–6
- [ ] Scheduled at evening slot (8–10 PM)

## Content Fatigue Rules
- Never post the same product type twice in a row
- Rotate pillars every day
- Repeat top-performing posts after 3–4 weeks, not sooner
- Watch engagement — if drops for 3 consecutive posts, change format
