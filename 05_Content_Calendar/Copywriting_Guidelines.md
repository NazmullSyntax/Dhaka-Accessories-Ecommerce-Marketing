# Copywriting Guidelines (Simulated)

## The 3-Part Formula
Every caption must have:
1. **Hook** — first line that stops scrolling
2. **Body** — benefits + at least one number (price, delivery, return)
3. **CTA** — clear action (Inbox, order, comment)

## The 5-Point Checklist
- [ ] Hook in first line
- [ ] Benefit stated, not just feature
- [ ] At least one number (price / days / quantity)
- [ ] Clear CTA
- [ ] Matches brand voice (friendly, respectful, simple Bangla)

| Kundan stone | "বিয়ের অনুষ্ঠানে premium look" |

| Laptop compartment | "ল্যাপটপ আর বই একসাথে বহন" |
| 6-month durability | "দীর্ঘদিন ব্যবহার করতে পারবেন" |

## Platform-Specific Rules
| Platform | Caption length | Emoji | CTA style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 3–5 lines | 2–3 | "Inbox করুন" |
| Instagram | 5–7 lines + hashtags | 3–4 | "DM us" / "Link in bio" |
| TikTok | 1–2 lines | 1–2 | "Order via link in bio" |

## Bangla Proofreading Checklist
- [ ] "আপনি" consistently used
- [ ] No spelling errors (use a Bangla dictionary if unsure)
- [ ] No ALL CAPS in Bangla
- [ ] Numbers in Bangla or English digits consistent (we use English digits: 650, 2, 7)
- [ ] No negative or pressure words from the "Words We Avoid" list
