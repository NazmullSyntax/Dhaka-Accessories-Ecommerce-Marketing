# Content Pillars — Dhaka Accessories (Simulated)

**Disclaimer:** This content calendar is simulated for portfolio purposes.

## The 4 Pillars
| Pillar | Purpose | Target % | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sell | Drive orders | 40% | "Kundan set মাত্র ৬৫০ টাকা" |
| Educate | Build trust and authority | 25% | "গয়না কীভাবে পরিষ্কার করবেন" |
| Entertain | Boost engagement | 20% | "আপনার পছন্দ কোনটা?" |
| Trust | Reduce buyer hesitation | 15% | "নুসরাত আপুর review" |

## Weekly Cadence
- 5 posts per week minimum
- 1 reel per week (highest reach)
- 2 stories per day (behind the scenes)
- 1 engagement post per week (poll, question)

## Posting Times (Bangladesh)
| Slot | Time | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 9–10 AM | Educate, tips |
| Afternoon | 1–2 PM | Sell, offers |
| Evening | 8–10 PM | Reels, entertainment |

Evening is peak scroll time in Bangladesh — keep the best content here.

## Visual Type by Pillar
| Pillar | Best Visual Type |
|---|---|
| Sell | Single product image + CTA |
| Educate | Carousel (multi-slide tips) |
| Entertain | Poll image, meme, reel |
| Trust | Customer photo, quote card |

## Hashtag Strategy
- 4–6 hashtags per post
- Mix of: product-specific (#kundanjewelry), local (#dhakaaccessories), generic (#jewelrylove)
- Do not spam 30 hashtags — it looks cheap
