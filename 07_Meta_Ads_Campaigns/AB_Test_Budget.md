## Expected Sample Size
At 200 BDT CPA target, 1,000 BDT per variant = 5 conversions.
This is below the ideal 25+ conversions [citation:7], so results are directional only.
To get 25+ conversions, budget would need to be 5,000 BDT per variant — outside our monthly budget.
Therefore, we test small and treat results as learning, not proof.
