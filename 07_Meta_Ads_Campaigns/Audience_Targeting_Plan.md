# Audience Targeting Plan — Dhaka Accessories (Simulated)

**Disclaimer:** Simulated targeting plan for portfolio purposes.

## The 3 Audience Layers

### Layer 1: Cold (Saved Audience)
People who do not know us. Reached via interest + demographic targeting.

| Audience Name | Details | Used For |
|---|---|---|
| AUD_Cold_Kundan_Women25-34 | Women, 25–34, Dhaka (25km), interests: jewelry, wedding, fashion accessories | C1 Kundan |
| AUD_Cold_Tote_Students | Men+Women, 22–30, Dhaka, interests: laptops, university, freelancing, bags | C2 Tote |

### Layer 2: Warm (Custom Audience — Engagement)
People who interacted with our page but did not message yet.

| Audience Name | Source | Retention | Used For |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUD_Warm_PageEngagers_30d | Page engagers (any interaction) | 30 days | Retargeting |
| AUD_Warm_VideoViewers_50% | People who watched 50%+ of our reels | 30 days | Retargeting |

### Layer 3: Hot (Custom Audience — Messaging)
People who messaged us but did not order.

| Audience Name | Source | Retention | Used For |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUD_Hot_Messengers_14d | People who sent a message | 14 days | C3 Retarget |

## Why This Structure
- Cold campaigns find new buyers
- Warm campaigns re-engage people who showed interest
- Hot campaigns close people who almost bought
- Each layer has a different CPA target (hot should be cheapest)

## Targeting Strategy for Bangladesh (2026)
| Factor | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Interest targeting | Yes, for cold | Small budget; helps algorithm learn faster |
| Broad targeting | Test later | Needs 50+ conversions/week to work; we don't have that yet |
| Lookalike | Later | No purchaser data yet |
| Location | Dhaka only | COD + 2-day delivery constraint |
| Age | 25–34 for jewelry, 22–30 for bag | Day 3 personas |
| Gender | Women for jewelry, All for bag | Product nature |

## Audience Rules
- Never overlap cold and retargeting in the same ad set
- Exclude purchasers from cold campaigns
- Refresh warm audiences monthly
- Frequency above 3 means audience too small — expand
