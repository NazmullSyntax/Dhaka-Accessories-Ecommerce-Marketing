|---|---|---|
| Campaign | Objective | Sales |

## Why These Choices
| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| Sales objective | We want orders, not just traffic |
| On-post conversion | Orders come via message, not website |
| Women 25–34 | Day 3 persona Nusrat |
| Dhaka only | Cash on delivery and fast delivery constraint |
| 1,500 BDT/day | Test budget within 15,000/month limit |
| Advantage+ placements | Let Meta optimize placement |
| Send message CTA | Direct path to order |
