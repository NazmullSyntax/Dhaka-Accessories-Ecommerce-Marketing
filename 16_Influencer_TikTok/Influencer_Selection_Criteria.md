# Influencer Selection Criteria (Simulated)

## Target Influencer Profile
| Criteria | Target |
|---|---|
| Follower range | 5,000–50,000 (Nano to Micro) |
| Engagement rate | ≥4% |
| Audience location | Dhaka, Bangladesh (60%+) |
| Audience age | 20–35 |
| Audience gender | 70%+ female (for jewelry) |
| Content niche | Fashion, lifestyle, beauty, bridal |
| Language | Bangla (primary), English mixed |
| Post frequency | 3+ per week |
| Brand safety | No controversy, no adult content |

## How to Verify
- **Fake follower check:** engagement rate ÷ follower count. If followers 50K but average likes 200, suspicious.
- **Audience location:** ask for screenshot of Instagram/Facebook insights, or check comment language
- **Content style:** review last 10 posts — are they consistent?
- **Sponsored ratio:** if 8 out of 10 posts are ads, audience tunes out

## Why These 5
- Two nano influencers cost only product (low risk)
- Two micro influencers have strong engagement (4.3–4.8%)
- One paid micro (beauty) gives reach at manageable cost
- Total cash outlay: 7,000 BDT (within budget)
- All are Dhaka-based, Bangla-speaking, brand-safe

## What to Avoid
- Macro influencers (100K+) — too expensive, low engagement on small budget
- Influencers with 20+ sponsored posts per month — audience is ad-blind
- Influencers whose audience is not Dhaka-based — delivery is a constraint
- Influencers with controversial content — brand risk
