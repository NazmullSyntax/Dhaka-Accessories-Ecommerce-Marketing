# Influencer ROI Analysis (Simulated)

**Disclaimer:** All data is simulated for portfolio purposes.

## Campaign Summary
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Influencers engaged | 5 (2 nano barter, 2 micro barter, 1 micro paid) |
| Cash spent | 7,000 BDT |
| Product cost (barter) | ~1,000 BDT |
| Total cost | 8,000 BDT |
| Total reach | 78,500 |
| Orders | 29 |
| Revenue | 18,850 BDT |
| CPA | 276 BDT |
| Margin per order (Kundan) | 270 BDT |
| Net profit | −174 BDT |

## What This Means
- **Direct ROI:** roughly break-even, slightly negative
- **But:** 29 new customers, 78,500 people saw the brand, content reusable in ads
- **Lifetime value:** if even half of 29 buy again, profit turns positive

## Why Direct ROI is Not the Only Measure
Influencer marketing builds:
1. **Brand awareness** — 78,500 people saw the brand
2. **Content library** — 5 reels reusable as ads for 60 days
3. **Social proof** — customer stories that reduce hesitation for new buyers
4. **Word of mouth** — influencer audience talks about the product

## What to Measure Going Forward
- **CPA** (cost per order)
- **Reach per taka spent** (efficiency)
- **Content reuse value** (how many ads use influencer reels)
- **Repeat purchase rate** (do influencer customers come back?)
- **Engagement rate** (is the audience actually engaging?)
