# TikTok Content Plan — Dhaka Accessories (Simulated)

## Why TikTok
TikTok is growing rapidly among 18–30 audiences in Bangladesh. Short, trend-driven videos get organic reach without ad spend. Our goal: 2 videos per week, 8 per month, tested for 60 days before deciding to scale.

## Content Mix (8 videos/month)

| Type | Count | Purpose | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unboxing | 2 | Show product close-up, build trust | 20–30 sec |
| Styling | 2 | Show versatility, save-worthy content | 20–25 sec |
| Trend-based | 3 | Use trending audio, ride the algorithm | 15 sec |
| Customer story | 1 | Social proof | 25–30 sec |

## Technical Rules
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (1080×1920)
- Length: 15–30 sec
- Text overlay: 3 per video
- Safe margin: 100 px from top and bottom
- Post time: 8–10 PM (Bangladesh)
- Trending audio check: every Monday

## What We Will NOT Do
- No inappropriate audio
- No misleading claims (do not say "original gold" if it is gold-plated)
- No copying competitor content exactly
- No paid TikTok ads yet — organic only for first 60 days

## Success Metrics (60-day test)
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Videos published | 16 |
| Average views | 1,000+ |
| Followers gained | 200+ |
| Inbox inquiries from TikTok | 15+ |
| Orders from TikTok | 5+ |

## What to Do After 60 Days
If targets met: scale to 3 videos/week, consider paid TikTok ads
If targets not met: reduce to 1/week, focus on Facebook/Instagram
