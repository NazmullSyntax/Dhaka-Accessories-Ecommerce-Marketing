# Dhaka Accessories — Business Brief (Simulated)

## Business Overview
Dhaka Accessories is a simulated B2C fashion accessories brand based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It sells affordable, trend-driven accessories (jewelry, bags, hair accessories) to young urban consumers.

**Disclaimer:** This is a learning and portfolio project. All data is simulated. This is not a real business and does not represent EasyBuy.com.bd or any real company's data.

## Target Market
- Primary location: Dhaka division (highest online shopping participation in Bangladesh at 21.3%) [BBS 2024-25]
- Age group: 25–34 (most active online shopping demographic at 24.7%) [BBS 2024-25]
- Gender: Both, with a skew toward female fashion buyers
- Income: Mid-range, price-sensitive

## Business Model
- Products sourced locally and sold via Facebook and Instagram (F-commerce)
- Payment: Cash on delivery (dominant at 86.8%) [BBS 2024-25], plus bKash
- Delivery: Third-party courier within Dhaka, 2–3 days

## Marketing Goals (for this project)
1. Build brand awareness on Facebook and Instagram
2. Generate 50+ orders per month (simulated)
3. Achieve ROAS of 3.0+ (simulated target)
4. Maintain a consistent 30-day content presence

## Key Constraints
- Limited ad budget (simulated: 15,000 BDT per month)
- Cash on delivery means order confirmation and return management matter
- Competition from Daraz, Facebook F-commerce pages, and local brands

## Project Scope
This project covers strategy, content, Meta Ads planning, Pixel tracking, GA4, data analysis, dashboard building, seasonal campaigns, and reporting — all using simulated data.
