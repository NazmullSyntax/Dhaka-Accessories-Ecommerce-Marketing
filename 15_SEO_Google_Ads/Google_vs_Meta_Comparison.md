# Google Ads vs Meta Ads — Dhaka Accessories Context (Simulated)

## When to Use Each

| Situation | Better Channel | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| New product launch | Meta Ads | People are not searching for it yet — we need to create demand |
| People searching "kundan set dhaka" | Google Ads | High intent — they are already looking |
| Brand awareness | Meta Ads | Visual, scroll-stopping, cheaper reach |
| High-intent purchase | Google Ads | Search query = buying signal |
| Retargeting page visitors | Both | Meta for social retarget, Google for search retarget |
| Seasonal sale (11.11) | Meta Ads | Urgency works better in feed |
| Always-on sales | Google Ads | Search demand exists year-round |

## Our Strategy (when budget allows)
1. **Start with Meta Ads** (current plan) — cheaper, visual, works for discovery
2. **Add Google Search** when we have a website and 50+ purchases/month — capture high-intent searches
3. **Add Google Shopping** when product feed is ready — image + price in search results [citation:1]
4. **SEO is always-on** — free traffic that compounds

## Key Difference for Bangladesh
- Meta Ads dominates because Facebook is the primary discovery platform
- Google Ads is growing as more people search directly for products
- Cash on delivery is the norm — both channels must emphasize COD in ads
- Messenger is the order channel — Meta Ads has a direct advantage here
