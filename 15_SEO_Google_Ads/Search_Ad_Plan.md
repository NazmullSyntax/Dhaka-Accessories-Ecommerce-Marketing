## Why This Structure
- 3 ads per ad group allows A/B testing of headlines [citation:4]
- Headlines include price and COD — the two strongest trust signals in Bangladesh
- Final URL is the Facebook page because orders close in Messenger
- Negative keywords prevent wasted spend on irrelevant searches
