# How to Present the Monthly Report to a Manager (Simulated)

## The 6-Minute Rule
A manager does not want to read 8 pages. Present in 6 minutes with this structure.

## Minute 1: Executive Summary
"This month we spent 15,050 BDT and generated 79,650 BDT revenue — ROAS 5.29, which is 176% of our target. Revenue and orders both exceeded target. Two campaigns are ready to scale, one needs a fix."

## Minute 2: KPI Snapshot
Show 4 numbers on screen (or the one-pager):
- Spend: 15,050 (100%)
- Revenue: 79,650 (177%)
- ROAS: 5.29 (176%)
- Orders: 125 (156%)

## Minute 3: Top Win
"11.11 was the best week — ROAS 6.50 and 47 orders. Retargeting had the highest efficiency at 6.93. The countdown creative structure is repeatable."

## Minute 4: Top Issue
"Tote bag underperformed — ROAS 3.49 and CPA 215. This is a creative-audience mismatch, not a product problem. The fix is a new creative and split audience test — not more budget."

## Minute 5: Next Month Plan
- Shift 900 BDT from Tote to Retarget (expected +1,500 revenue)
- Build Eid campaign on 11.11 structure (reserve 6,000 BDT)
- Rotate creatives every 5–7 days (CTR improvement)

## Minute 6: Ask
"Approval needed: budget reallocation as proposed, and Eid campaign brief review by end of next week."

## Anticipated Questions and Answers
**Q: Why not scale Tote more if revenue is positive?**
A: "ROAS 3.49 is profitable but our Retarget earns 6.93. Every taka is better spent on Retarget until Tote's creative is fixed. The budget shift increases total revenue without increasing total spend."

**Q: Can we hit ROAS 7 next month?**
A: "Only with a bigger seasonal campaign. Without 11.11, ROAS tends toward 5–5.5. Eid is our next spike — that's where 6+ becomes realistic."

**Q: Why did W5 drop?**
A: "Post-11.11 cooldown. Demand was pulled forward into the sale. This is normal and expected; the fix is retargeting the 11.11 engagers in W5, which we plan to do."

**Q: What is our biggest risk next month?**
A: "Eid CPM will rise 40–120% as other brands compete. If Eid budget is not reserved early, we will overspend on cold traffic. That's why 6,000 BDT is ring-fenced."

## What Not to Do
- Do not show 20 charts — 4 KPIs and 2 charts is enough
- Do not blame team members
- Do not end without a specific ask (approval, budget, timeline)
- Do not skip the disclaimer — this is simulated data
