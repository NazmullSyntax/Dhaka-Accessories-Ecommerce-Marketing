## 5. Key Learnings

### Learning 1: Seasonal campaigns beat baseline
The 11.11 week delivered 6.50 ROAS vs 5.29 monthly average. Seasonality lifts both volume and efficiency. The countdown structure (teaser → launch → last chance) worked.

### Learning 2: Retargeting is the cheapest revenue
Retarget ROAS 6.93 on 1,500 BDT spend. Warm audiences need less persuasion. Every taka spent on retargeting outperforms cold by roughly 40%.

### Learning 3: Tote bag needs product-market-fit fix, not more budget
Tote ROAS 3.49 with the highest CPA 215 BDT. More budget will not fix this. The issue is creative-audience mismatch, not spend.

### Learning 4: CTR is our weak metric
Monthly CTR 1.89% vs 2% target. The same creatives ran for 8–14 days without refresh. Creative rotation every 5–7 days is needed.

### Learning 5: Return rate changes the profit picture
At 20% return rate, adjusted profit is 20,110 BDT on 15,050 BDT spend — still healthy, but ROAS alone would overstate the result.

## 6. Strategic Recommendations for Next Month

**Rationale:** Retarget ROAS 6.93 vs Tote 3.49. Every taka in retargeting earns nearly twice as much.
**Expected impact:** +1,500 BDT revenue at same total spend.

### Recommendation 2: Refresh Tote creative with new audience angle
**Action:** Test "student-friendly" review image + separate audiences (university vs freelancing). 7-day A/B test.
**Rationale:** Tote has the lowest CTR 1.58% and highest CPA 215. The product itself is fine (margin 61.3%) — the creative-audience pairing is wrong.
**Expected impact:** If CPA improves to 180, ROAS moves from 3.49 to 4.17.

### Recommendation 3: Apply 11.11 structure to Eid campaign
**Action:** Build Eid campaign (5-day teaser → launch → last day) starting 3 weeks before Eid. Reserve 40% of budget (6,000 BDT).
**Rationale:** 11.11 structure delivered 6.50 ROAS. Same urgency mechanic applies to Eid.
**Expected impact:** Target ROAS 6.0, target 50 orders.

### Recommendation 4: Increase creative rotation frequency
**Action:** Rotate creatives every 5–7 days. Always keep 2 active ad variants per ad set.
**Rationale:** CTR 1.89% vs 2% target is directly caused by creative fatigue.
**Expected impact:** CTR +0.3%, CPA down 5–10%.

### Recommendation 5: Scale Retarget audience window from 30 to 60 days
**Action:** Expand page engager window to 60 days. Add video viewer audience (50%+ watches).
**Rationale:** Retarget is our best performer but audience is small. Expanding warm pool allows scaling without frequency spikes.
**Expected impact:** Retarget orders +20% at same ROAS.
