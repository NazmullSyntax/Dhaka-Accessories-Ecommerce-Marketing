# Portfolio Presentation Script (3-Minute Version)

## Opening (20 seconds)
"Hi, I'm [Name]. Over the last 30 days I built a complete e-commerce marketing project for a simulated brand called Dhaka Accessories. It covers strategy, content, Meta Ads planning, analytics, and dashboarding — everything a Digital Marketing Executive does day to day."

## Problem (30 seconds)
"Dhaka Accessories is a fictional B2C fashion accessories brand in Dhaka. The challenge: brand new, small 15,000 BDT monthly budget, and a market where 86.8% of shoppers pay cash on delivery. Fashion is the #1 online category in Bangladesh, so the opportunity is real — but only if the marketing is disciplined."

## Strategy (30 seconds)
"I started with a business brief, two customer personas — Nusrat, a 27-year-old bank executive buying wedding jewelry, and Tanvir, a 24-year-old student buying a laptop bag — and a competitor analysis of three brands. That gave me a positioning: fast delivery, COD, and bundle offers — something no direct competitor combines."

## Content & Ads (40 seconds)
"I built a 30-day content calendar with four pillars — sell, educate, entertain, trust — at a 40/25/20/15 ratio. I designed three Canva ad creatives and wrote five reels scripts. For ads, I planned four Meta campaigns with distinct audiences — cold Kundan, cold Tote, retarget, and a 10-day 11.11 campaign — plus a three-test A/B plan covering creative, audience, and copy."

## Analytics (30 seconds)
"I built a 30-day simulated dataset with 40 rows and 19 columns, then analyzed it in Google Sheets for CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, and ROAS. I turned that into a Looker Studio dashboard with four scorecards, a time-series, a funnel, and a campaign table."

## Results (30 seconds)
"In the simulated data, total spend was 15,050 BDT, revenue 79,650 BDT, ROAS 5.29, 125 orders. Retarget was the best performer at ROAS 6.93. Tote underperformed at 3.49 — but it is still profitable; it just needs a creative fix, not more budget."

## Learnings & Close (20 seconds)
"Three learnings: retargeting is the cheapest revenue, seasonal campaigns dominate, and creative rotation matters more than budget increases. My next steps would be to shift budget from Tote to Retarget and build the Eid campaign on the 11.11 structure. The full project is on GitHub — link is on the last slide. Thank you."

---

## What to Say If Asked "Is This Real Data?"
"This is simulated data for portfolio purposes. I built it to practice the full analysis workflow without spending real ad money. The patterns are realistic — retarget has higher ROAS than cold, seasonal campaigns spike, some creatives fatigue — but the numbers are not from a real business."

## What Not to Say
- Do not claim real business results
- Do not claim 1–3 years of experience you do not have
- Do not skip the disclaimer
- Do not read the slides word for word — talk to the recruiter

## Timing Check
- Opening: 20 sec
- Problem: 30 sec
- Strategy: 30 sec
- Content & Ads: 40 sec
- Analytics: 30 sec
- Results: 30 sec
- Learnings & Close: 20 sec
- **Total: 3 minutes 20 seconds**

Practice 3 times out loud before the interview.
