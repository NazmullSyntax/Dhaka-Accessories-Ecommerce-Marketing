## 1. Campaign Overview
A 5-week Eid campaign built around Ramadan and Eid-ul-Fitr. Three phases — pre-Ramadan teaser, Ramadan showcase + countdown, post-Eid thank-you. Eid is the largest shopping season in Bangladesh, so the campaign is longer and more culturally layered than 11.11.

## 2. Business Goal
- **Primary:** 100+ confirmed orders during the campaign
- **Secondary:** 300+ new followers, 500+ Messenger conversations
- **Revenue target:** 70,000 BDT (at ~700 BDT AOV, higher than 11.11 because of bundles)

## 3. Target Audience

| Layer | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Women 25–40, Dhaka, Eid shopping interest | Gift + self-purchase |
| Secondary | Men 25–40 buying gifts for family | Gift angle |
| Warm | Page engagers from last 60 days | Retarget with bundle offer |
| Family | Existing customers | Repeat + gifting |

**Why 25–40 instead of 25–34:** Eid shopping often includes older family members and gift purchases, so the audience is slightly broader than regular campaigns.

## 4. Offer & Pricing

### Eid Bundle Offers
- **Family Set:** Kundan choker + pearl earrings = 900 BDT (was 1,000 → 10% off)
- **Gift Set:** Bangle + earrings in gift box = 750 BDT (was 900 → 17% off)
- **Bride Bundle:** Full jewelry set + tote bag = 1,300 BDT (was 1,400 → 7% off)

### Individual Discounts
- 15–25% off across individual SKUs (lower than 11.11 because bundle is the main offer)
- Free delivery inside Dhaka for orders above 1,000 BDT
- COD available

**Why bundles over flat discount:**
Eid is a gifting season — customers buy for others. A flat 30% discount would cut margin without lifting AOV. Bundles lift AOV from 650 to 900+, so even at 10% discount, total profit per order is higher.

## 10. Cultural Sensitivity Rules

- **Never use religious imagery** (mosque, Quran, prayer) in ads — focus on celebration, family, fashion
- **"Eid Mubarak"** is appropriate in greetings, not as a sales push
- **Avoid aggressive urgency** in the first two weeks of Ramadan (fasting hours) — people are focused on spiritual matters
- **Use warm, respectful language** — "collection" not "sale", "gift" not "grab"
- **After Iftar (sunset)** is the best posting time during Ramadan — peak scroll hours shift to 9–11 PM
- **Do not discount below margin** even during peak — the offer should be bundles, not blanket cuts
