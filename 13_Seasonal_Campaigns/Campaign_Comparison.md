|---|---|---|---|

## Why These Differences Matter

### Duration
Eid needs 5 weeks because Ramadan changes daily routines (fasting, Iftar, prayer). Ad fatigue sets in faster; need more creative variety. 11.11 is a 10-day sprint. Black Friday is a 4-day flash.

### Budget allocation
- Eid: highest total (8,000) because largest season
- 11.11: medium (6,000) because only 10 days
- Black Friday: smallest (2,500) because audience size is smaller in Bangladesh

### Peak concentration
All three concentrate budget on the peak day/week — do not spread evenly. Peak = highest intent = highest ROAS.

### Audience age
- Eid is broader (25–40) because gift-buying
- 11.11 is standard e-commerce (25–34)
- Black Friday is younger (20–30) because deal-hunting culture is urban youth

## Lessons Applied from 11.11 to Eid and Black Friday
1. **Countdown works** — apply to Eid's last week and Black Friday's 48 hours
2. **Creative rotation** — every 2 days during peak
3. **Contingency triggers** — CPM, CPA, stock-out thresholds pre-defined
4. **Post-campaign retargeting** — Eid engagers become Black Friday warm audience
5. **Budget ring-fencing** — reserve seasonal budget separately from monthly
