## 1. Campaign Overview
A short, high-intensity 4-day Black Friday campaign. Black Friday is a growing trend in Bangladesh, especially among urban 20–30 audiences. Unlike Eid, this is not a cultural event — it is a deal-hunting moment. Deep discounts, aggressive urgency, and 48-hour flash sale are expected.

## 2. Business Goal
- **Primary:** 25+ confirmed orders over 4 days
- **Secondary:** 80+ new followers, 150+ Messenger conversations
- **Revenue target:** 15,000 BDT (at ~600 BDT AOV)

## 3. Target Audience

| Layer | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Men + Women 20–30, Dhaka, deal hunters | Main buyer |
| Warm | Page engagers last 30 days | Retarget with flash offer |
| Lookalike | Similar to prior purchasers | Expand cold pool |

**Note:** Black Friday audience is younger than Eid audience. Deal-seeking behavior peaks in the 20–30 age group.

## 4. Offer & Pricing

### Black Friday Deals
- **Flat 30% off** on all SKUs (deep discount is expected — anything less looks fake)
- **Flash deal (Friday only):** Extra 10% on first 20 orders
- **Free delivery inside Dhaka** for all orders
- COD available

## 10. Cultural Sensitivity Notes

- Black Friday is **not** a religious or cultural event in Bangladesh — copy can be direct and deal-focused
- Target younger, urban audiences who are familiar with Western shopping trends
- Do not use terms like "Black Friday" in Bengali transliteration that might confuse older audiences (e.g., "ব্ল্যাক ফ্রাইডে" is fine, but explanation helps)
- Emphasize **COD** and **fast delivery** — trust signals matter even in flash sales
