# 11.11 Budget Plan (Simulated)

## Total Budget: 6,000 BDT
Reserved from monthly 15,000 BDT (40%). Ring-fenced so regular campaigns do not eat into it.

## Phase Allocation

| Phase | Days | Budget | Daily | % | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teaser | 3 | 1,200 | 400 | 20% | Reach + engagement |
| Countdown | 2 | 1,200 | 600 | 20% | Messages |
| Launch Day | 1 | 2,700 | 2,700 | 45% | Messages + orders |
| Last Chance + Recap | 4 | 900 | 225 | 15% | Re-engagement |
| **Total** | **10** | **6,000** | **600 avg** | **100%** | |

## Budget Type
Use **Lifetime budget** (6,000 BDT) with end date 15-Dec. This prevents Meta from over-spending on a single day and spreads the budget across the 10 days.

## Expected Results
| Metric | Target | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | 40+ | At CPA 150, 6,000 / 150 = 40 |
| Revenue | 24,000 BDT | At AOV 600 |
| ROAS | 4.0 | 24,000 / 6,000 |
| CPA | ≤150 | Target |

## Scenario Planning

### Best Case (ROAS 6.0)
- Orders: 60
- Revenue: 36,000
- Profit (at 40% margin after returns): ~14,400

### Expected Case (ROAS 4.0)
- Orders: 40
- Revenue: 24,000
- Profit: ~9,600

### Worst Case (ROAS 2.0)
- Orders: 20
- Revenue: 12,000
- Profit: ~4,800

**Break-even ROAS for 11.11 mixed SKUs: ~1.70. Even worst case is profitable.**

## Budget Protection Rules
- Do not increase budget more than 20% per day
- If CPM rises above 250 BDT, reduce cold audience budget 30%
- If CPA above 200 for 2 days, pause that ad set
- Keep 10% (600 BDT) untouched as emergency reserve for the final day
