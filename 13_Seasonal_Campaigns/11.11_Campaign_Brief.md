## 1. Campaign Overview
A 10-day seasonal campaign built around the 11.11 sale window. Four phases — teaser, countdown, launch, last chance + recap — with phase-wise budget, creative rotation, and audience layering.

## 2. Business Goal
- **Primary:** 40+ confirmed orders during the campaign
- **Secondary:** 100+ new page followers, 200+ Messenger conversations
- **Revenue target:** 24,000 BDT (at ~600 BDT AOV)

## 3. Target Audience
| Layer | Audience | Size | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold | Broad Dhaka, 22–35, shopping interests | Largest | New buyers via countdown creatives |
| Warm | Page engagers (30 days) | Medium | Retarget with launch offer |
| Hot | Prior messengers | Small | Last-chance close |

## 4. Offer & Pricing
- **Discount:** 20–40% off across all SKUs
- **Bundle:** Buy 2 sets get 10% additional off
- **COD available** on all orders
- **Free delivery inside Dhaka** for orders above 900 BDT
- **Return:** 7-day policy as usual

**Why this offer:**
- 20–40% is deep enough to trigger urgency but preserves margin (Kundan margin 61.5% → after 30% discount, margin 46%)
- Bundle increases AOV, offsets discount
- Free delivery above 900 pushes AOV to 900+

## 11. Contingency Plan

| If this happens | Do this |
|---|---|
| CPM rises above 250 BDT | Reduce cold audience budget by 30%, shift to retarget |
| CTR falls below 1.5% | Pause current creative, launch new variant same day |
| Stock runs out on a SKU | Pause ads for that SKU within 1 hour, redirect budget |
| CPA above 200 BDT for 2 days | Pause ad set, review audience + creative, relaunch with fix |
| Messenger reply time above 30 min | Activate saved replies, assign backup for evening shift |
| Return rate above 30% | Pause cold campaigns, focus on retarget, add confirmation call |
