# Next 30 Days — Learning and Job Search Plan

## Goal
Get a Digital Marketing Executive role in Dhaka within 90 days.

## Week 1–2: Apply + Practice
| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Apply to 5 jobs per day | Daily |
| Send 2 LinkedIn connection requests to marketing managers | Daily |
| Practice 1 mock interview | 3 per week |
| Update portfolio deck based on feedback | Once |

## Week 3–4: Real Spend (if possible)
| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Offer a friend's small business 2 weeks of free ad management | 5,000 BDT budget, they pay for ad spend |
| Build a real 30-day dataset from that campaign | Compare with simulated |
| Write a real performance report | Add to portfolio as "Real Campaign (small budget)" |

## Week 5–8: Skill Gaps
| Skill | Resource | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (live) | Google Skillshop (free) | 10 hours |
| SEO (on-page) | Ahrefs blog (free) | 5 hours |
| GA4 (live implementation) | Google Analytics Academy | 8 hours |
| Meta Blueprint certification | Facebook Blueprint (free) | 15 hours |

## Week 9–12: Interview Loop
| Task | Target |
|---|---|
| Applications sent | 60+ |
| First-round interviews | 10+ |
| Final-round interviews | 3+ |
| Offers | 1+ |

## Weekly Routine
- **Monday:** 5 applications, review last week's rejections
- **Tuesday:** 1 mock interview, refine weakest answer
- **Wednesday:** 5 applications, 1 LinkedIn conversation
- **Thursday:** Skill practice (Google Ads or SEO)
- **Friday:** 5 applications, update tracker
- **Saturday:** Read 1 industry article, add to portfolio if useful
- **Sunday:** Rest, plan next week

## What Not to Do
- Do not apply to 100 jobs in one day and then wait
- Do not skip follow-up after 5–7 days
- Do not claim experience you do not have
- Do not stop learning while waiting for interviews
- Do not compare your Day 30 with someone else's Year 3
